# Due diligence: MICROSOFT CORP (MSFT)

CIK 789019 · 10-K filed 2026-07-29 · accession 0001193125-26-323660

**Overall risk: LOW.** Revenue grew from $245.1B to $331.8B over three years, and net income grew from $88.1B to $133.7B. Liabilities of $316.0B are well below assets of $758.4B, and long-term debt is $40.3B and falling. Management and Deloitte both found internal control over financial reporting effective. Searches found no going-concern, restatement, material weakness or auditor-change language. The main risks are the size of the AI capital spending, supply and power constraints, the $28.9B IRS dispute, cybersecurity, and competition and regulation. Item 3 only points to Note 14, which I did not read, so I could not confirm the litigation picture.

## Financial flags

- **Low:** Cash fell to $20.935B at FY2026 from $30.242B at FY2025 (about -31%), while total assets rose to $758.376B from $619.003B. Why: The asset base is growing fast, consistent with heavy datacenter and AI investment. Cash is still large next to long-term debt of $40.294B, so liquidity looks adequate. The 10-K does not show whether the cash decline is a deliberate shift into investments.
- **Low:** Total assets grew about 22% in FY2026, faster than revenue growth of about 18%. Why: Capital intensity is rising. Returns on the AI buildout depend on future demand.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **Medium · financial:** The AI and cloud buildout needs large and rising capital spending ahead of revenue. Returns are uncertain and depend on access to capital. "in advance of fully developed revenue streams. The associated revenue may not be realized in the expected timeframes or at expected levels." [1A-p020]
- **Medium · operational:** Datacenter expansion faces power, component and supply constraints that could limit growth. "We have experienced and may continue to experience supply constraints, including shortages of semiconductors, networking equipment, power systems, cooling equipment" [1A-p068]
- **Medium · legal:** The IRS is seeking $28.9B plus penalties and interest over transfer pricing for 2004–2013. Microsoft disputes it and says its allowances are adequate. An adverse outcome could be material. "the IRS is seeking an additional tax payment of $28.9 billion plus penalties and interest." [7-p133]
- **Medium · operational:** A nation-state attack on Microsoft's systems exposed email accounts and some source code repositories. Further attacks could harm customers and reputation. "a nation-state associated threat actor used a password spray attack to compromise a legacy test account and, in turn, gain access to Microsoft email accounts." [1A-p040]
- **Medium · market:** AI is highly competitive. Some competitors and frontier model providers are also partners and customers, so the relationships may shift. "Our AI offerings compete with AI products from hyperscalers, open-source offerings, and frontier model providers, some of which are also current or potential partners." [1A-p014]
- **Medium · regulatory:** Laws on AI, data and other areas are changing and sometimes conflict across jurisdictions. Compliance could be costly, and noncompliance could bring penalties. "Compliance with existing, expanding, or new laws and regulations may involve significant costs and operational efforts" [1A-p087]

## Open questions for a reviewer

- Check capital expenditure and free cash flow trends, and why cash fell about 31% in FY2026.
- Assess how much of the $28.9B IRS exposure is reserved, and the timing of any resolution.
- Review customer and partner concentration in AI and Azure, including the frontier model relationships.
- Review the auditor's critical audit matters and the Item 9A text beyond the paragraphs read.
