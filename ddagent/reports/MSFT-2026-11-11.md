# Due diligence: MICROSOFT CORP (MSFT)

CIK 789019 · 10-K filed 2026-07-29 · accession 0001193125-26-323660

**Overall risk: LOW.** Revenue grew from $245.1B to $331.8B over three years, and net income grew from $88.1B to $133.7B. Liabilities are about 42% of assets and long-term debt is falling. Controls were assessed as effective, with a clean Deloitte ICFR opinion (9A-p004). The searches found no going-concern doubt, material weakness, restatement or auditor change. Item 3 only refers to Note 14, which I did not read, so major litigation is marked not found, not ruled out. The main risks are the size of AI capital spending, supply and power limits, the $28.9B IRS dispute, antitrust scrutiny and cybersecurity. This is research, not investment advice.

## Financial flags

- **Low:** Cash fell to $20.935B in FY2026 from $30.242B in FY2025, while total assets grew to $758.376B from $619.003B. Why: Assets are growing quickly, consistent with heavy datacenter and AI investment. The cash balance is still large against long-term debt of $40.294B. Short-term investments are not in the data provided.
- **Low:** Total assets rose about 22% in FY2026, faster than revenue growth of about 18%. Why: Capital intensity is rising. Returns depend on AI and cloud demand materializing as expected.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **Medium · financial:** AI and cloud buildout requires large and rising capital spending ahead of revenue. Revenue may arrive later or lower than expected, and the build depends on continued access to financing. "in advance of fully developed revenue streams. The associated revenue may not be realized in the expected timeframes or at expected levels." [1A-p020]
- **Medium · operational:** Datacenter expansion depends on power availability and scarce components, such as semiconductors. Both limit capacity growth. "We have experienced and may continue to experience supply constraints, including shortages of semiconductors, networking equipment, power systems, cooling equipment" [1A-p068]
- **Medium · operational:** Microsoft has had cybersecurity incidents, including a nation-state breach that reached source code repositories and internal systems. Further attacks could harm its reputation and customer relationships. "gain unauthorized access to some of our source code repositories and internal systems" [1A-p040]
- **Medium · legal:** The IRS is seeking $28.9B plus penalties and interest for tax years 2004–2013, over transfer pricing. An adverse outcome could materially affect results. "the IRS is seeking an additional tax payment of $28.9 billion plus penalties and interest." [1A-p091]
- **Medium · regulatory:** Antitrust and digital-market regulators in several jurisdictions are scrutinizing Microsoft. This could lead to fines or force changes to products. "U.S. and foreign antitrust authorities have previously brought enforcement actions and continue to scrutinize our business." [1A-p080]
- **Medium · market:** Competition in AI and cloud is intense. Some AI partners are also competitors and Azure customers, so their demand may change. "many of these partners compete with us with respect to certain products and services." [1A-p023]
