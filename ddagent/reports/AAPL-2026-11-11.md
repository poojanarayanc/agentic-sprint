# Due diligence: Apple Inc. (AAPL)

CIK 320193 · 10-K filed 2026-11-03 · accession 0000320193-26-000026

**Overall risk: LOW.** The financials are strong. Revenue grew from $391.0B to $478.8B over three years, net income rose from $93.7B to $132.2B, and long-term debt fell. Item 9A says internal control over financial reporting is effective, with Ernst & Young issuing the audit report. I found no going-concern, restatement, material-weakness or auditor-change language. The main concerns are antitrust and regulatory exposure (DOJ, EU DMA, Epic), supply concentration and tariffs. These could erode margins and services revenue but do not threaten solvency. The overall rating is low risk.

## Financial flags

- **Low:** Liabilities are large relative to assets: $292.5B of $404.5B total assets in FY2026, although this ratio has improved from $308.0B of $365.0B in FY2024. Why: Leverage is high in absolute terms, but earnings and cash generation are strong and long-term debt fell from $96.7B to $78.1B over the period.
- **Low:** Reported cash is $36.3B against $78.1B of long-term debt. The figures do not include marketable securities. Why: The cash line alone understates liquidity. A reviewer should check total cash plus marketable securities before drawing conclusions.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **High · legal:** The DOJ and state attorneys general have filed an antitrust monopolization suit over smartphones, and related private suits seek damages. The DOJ seeks equitable relief, which could force changes to business practices. "The DOJ is seeking equitable relief to redress the alleged anticompetitive behavior." [3-p005]
- **High · regulatory:** The EU fined Apple €500M under the DMA and issued a cease-and-desist order. A separate investigation (Article 6(4)) could bring fines of up to 10% of worldwide net sales. "it can issue a cease and desist order and may impose fines up to 10% of the Company’s annual worldwide net sales" [3-p002]
- **Medium · legal:** The Epic Games injunction bars commissions on external-link purchases. The Ninth Circuit allowed some commission, and the Supreme Court has taken the case. This threatens App Store services economics. "the Company can charge some commission on link-out purchases" [3-p006]
- **High · operational:** Supply is concentrated in Asia and relies on single or limited sources. Memory, storage and advanced semiconductor constraints and cost increases are expected to intensify. "The Company expects these trends to intensify for memory and storage components." [1A-p033]
- **Medium · market:** New US tariffs and retaliatory measures create uncertain cost and supply chain impacts, and tariff refunds may not be recoverable. "The ultimate impact remains uncertain" [1A-p013]
- **Medium · operational:** Heavy AI and cloud compute needs depend on third-party providers and large data center investment, raising capital expenditure and obligations. "There are limited third-party providers that can reliably meet such demand" [1A-p035]
- **Medium · operational:** AI is increasing the scale and speed of cybersecurity threats, and the company's own AI use adds exposure. "The rapid advancement and widespread dissemination of artificial intelligence technologies significantly increases the risks associated with cybersecurity attacks." [1A-p071]

## Red flags

## Open questions for a reviewer

- Quantify how much App Store and services revenue is exposed to commission changes from the Epic ruling and the EU DMA.
- Check total cash plus marketable securities and the debt maturity schedule, since the cash figure excludes marketable securities.
- Check the timeline and likely remedies in the DOJ antitrust case, and any accrued amounts for the €500M EU fine.
- Assess the tariff cost impact and memory/storage cost trends on gross margin.
- Review AI capital expenditure and cloud commitments in the notes.
