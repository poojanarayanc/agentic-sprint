# Due diligence: Natics Corp. (NTCS)

CIK 1933359 · 10-K filed 2026-06-09 · accession 0001683168-26-004650

**Overall risk: HIGH.** This is a micro-cap shell-like start-up (FY2026 revenue $27,600). It has a going concern opinion, a confirmed material weakness, and liabilities of $93,508 against assets of $8,866. It has lost money in every year shown, and it depends on advances from its sole executive. No litigation, restatement or auditor change turned up in the sections searched, but the financial fragility and governance gaps put it at high risk.

## Financial flags

- **High:** Liabilities ($93,508) are over 10x total assets ($8,866) at 4/30/2026; stockholders' deficit of roughly $84.6K (derived). Why: Balance sheet is deeply insolvent on a book basis, and the gap has widened each year (assets fell from $49,518 in FY2024).
- **High:** Net losses in all three years (-$12,160, -$41,049, -$27,159) with operating cash burn of $22,859 in FY2026, against tiny assets. Why: Very little runway; operations are funded by advances from the sole executive and equity.
- **Medium:** Revenue is tiny and volatile ($21,920, $16,200, $27,600); expenses of $54,759 are about twice revenue. Why: There is no sign of a self-sustaining business model.
- **Low:** Cash and long-term debt are not available from the data source. Why: Liquidity can't be verified from structured data. Liabilities are likely mostly related-party advances, but this was not confirmed.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **High · financial:** Auditors have issued a going concern opinion. The company is development stage with limited revenue and no assurance that financing will be available. "Our auditors have issued a going concern opinion." [7-p001]
- **High · financial:** Operations are funded by advances from the sole executive or by equity issuance. Future equity financing would dilute shareholders, and financing may not be available. "We have financed our operations primarily from either advances from our sole executive or the issuance of equity." [7-p015]
- **High · governance:** Internal control over financial reporting is not effective. There is a lack of segregation of duties and no independent directors or audit committee. "none of whom are independent under applicable standards; we do not have an audit committee or compensation committee." [9A-p011]
- **Medium · operational:** The company is a start-up with no meaningful operating history and depends on a sole executive. Management says the control weakness may not be remediated until operations commence. "We may not be able to fully remediate the material weakness until we commence operations" [9A-p013]
- **Low · regulatory:** Item 1A risk factors were omitted under smaller reporting company rules, so investors get little formal risk disclosure. "Not required for Smaller reporting companies." [1A-p001]

## Open questions for a reviewer

- What makes up the $93,508 of liabilities? Are they related-party loans from the sole officer, and are repayment terms disclosed?
- What is the year-end cash balance? The data source returned null.
- Is the sole officer able and willing to keep funding operations, and is there any plan to raise capital?
- Who is the auditor, and has it changed? Item 9 was not covered by the tools.
- The MD&A describes investing cash flows as both 'positive' and 'used'. Check the cash flow statement, along with the capitalized app/website costs, for impairment.
- Is there any change-of-control or reverse-merger risk typical of tiny shells? Review the shareholder base and the filings list.
