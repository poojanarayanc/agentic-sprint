# Due diligence: MICROSOFT CORP (MSFT)

CIK 789019 · 10-K filed 2026-07-29 · accession 0001193125-26-323660

**Overall risk: LOW.** Revenue grew from $245.1B to $331.8B over three years and net income from $88.1B to $133.7B. Long-term debt is modest at $40.3B against $758.4B of assets. Management and Deloitte both report effective internal controls (9A-p004, 9A-p008). Searches found no going concern, material weakness, restatement or auditor change. The main concerns are the scale and payback of AI capex, power and supply constraints, cybersecurity and regulation. These are business risks, not signs of financial distress. This is research, not investment advice.

## Financial flags

- **Low:** Cash fell from $30.242B (FY2025) to $20.935B (FY2026) while total assets rose from $619.003B to $758.376B. Why: Asset growth alongside lower cash is consistent with heavy datacenter/AI investment; worth monitoring liquidity and funding needs. Short-term investments were not returned by the tool.
- **Low:** Total liabilities rose from $243.686B (FY2024) to $315.989B (FY2026), although long-term debt declined from $44.937B to $40.294B. Why: Liability growth is coming from items other than long-term debt (e.g., possibly leases and other obligations); composition should be checked.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **Medium · financial:** Large, accelerated AI/cloud capex is ahead of revenue and depends on continued access to capital; returns may be delayed or lower than expected. "require substantial and increasing capital expenditures and continued access to capital, and are in advance of fully developed revenue streams" [1A-p020]
- **Medium · operational:** Datacenter expansion is constrained by electrical power availability and cost. "Limitations in power availability, delays in obtaining power connections, outages, shortages, increased energy costs" [1A-p065]
- **Medium · operational:** Supply constraints for semiconductors and other critical components, with competitors using the same suppliers. "We have experienced and may continue to experience supply constraints, including shortages of semiconductors, networking equipment, power systems" [1A-p068]
- **High · operational:** Cybersecurity exposure, including a prior nation-state compromise of corporate email and source code repositories that may continue to harm reputation and customer relationships. "a nation-state associated threat actor used a password spray attack to compromise a legacy test account" [1A-p040]
- **Medium · market:** AI strategy depends on partners that also compete with Microsoft and are significant Azure customers, so expected consumption may not materialize. "In some cases, these parties are significant customers of Azure and other cloud services." [1A-p023]
- **Medium · market:** Intense competition across cloud, AI and platforms from hyperscalers, open-source and frontier model providers. "Our AI offerings compete with AI products from hyperscalers, open-source offerings, and frontier model providers" [1A-p014]
- **Medium · regulatory:** Expanding and conflicting privacy, AI, and data laws globally may bring costs, product changes, or penalties. "Noncompliance could result in the imposition of penalties, criminal sanctions, or orders to cease the alleged noncompliant activity." [1A-p087]
