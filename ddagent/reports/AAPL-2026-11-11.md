# Due diligence: Apple Inc. (AAPL)

CIK 320193 · 10-K filed 2026-11-03 · accession 0000320193-26-000026

**Overall risk: LOW.** Financials are very strong: revenue grew from 391.0B to 478.8B and net income from 93.7B to 132.2B over three years. Long-term debt is falling and cash is 36.3B. Controls were assessed as effective (9A-p010), with Ernst & Young issuing an audit report on internal control. No going-concern, restatement or material-weakness language turned up in my search. The main risks are antitrust and regulatory exposure (DOJ, EU DMA, Epic), tariffs and supply-chain concentration. These could hurt margins and Services economics but do not threaten solvency. This is research, not investment advice.

## Financial flags

- **Low:** Liabilities are large (292.5B vs assets 404.5B), but long-term debt fell from 96.7B to 78.1B over three years. Why: Leverage is declining and is well covered by net income of 132.2B and growing revenue; not a solvency concern.
- **Low:** Total assets rose about 45B in FY2026 while liabilities rose only about 7B. Why: Equity increased materially; worth understanding drivers (cash flow, buybacks, securities), but it is not a negative signal.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **High · legal:** DOJ antitrust monopolization suit over smartphones, plus related private civil suits seeking damages; relief sought could alter the business model. "alleging monopolization or attempted monopolization in the markets for “performance smartphones” and “smartphones” in violation of U.S. antitrust laws" [3-p005]
- **High · regulatory:** EU DMA investigations: a €500M fine already imposed, and a possible fine of up to 10% of worldwide net sales in the Article 6(4) probe. "may impose fines up to 10% of the Company’s annual worldwide net sales" [3-p002]
- **Medium · legal:** Epic Games injunction bars commissions on out-of-app purchases, which pressures App Store economics. The Supreme Court has granted review on the civil contempt standard. "enjoined the Company from imposing any commission or any fee on purchases that consumers make outside an app" [3-p006]
- **Medium · market:** Tariffs and trade restrictions affect a supply chain concentrated in Asia. The outcome is uncertain and tariff refunds may not be collectible. "If tariffs are subsequently rescinded or revoked, the Company may not be able to successfully claim and collect tariff refunds." [1A-p013]
- **Medium · operational:** Manufacturing is concentrated in a few Asian countries, with single or limited sources for critical components. "particularly in regions where the Company relies on single or limited sources for critical components, such as advanced semiconductors, memory and storage" [1A-p012]
- **Medium · financial:** Gross margin is under pressure from rising component costs and supply constraints. "industry-wide supply constraints and rising costs for key components such as advanced semiconductors, memory and storage" [1A-p118]
