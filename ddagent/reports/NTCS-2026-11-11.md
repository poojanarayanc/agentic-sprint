# Due diligence: Natics Corp. (NTCS)

CIK 1933359 · 10-K filed 2026-06-09 · accession 0001683168-26-004650

**Overall risk: HIGH.** Micro-scale shell-like start-up with a going concern opinion, a confirmed material weakness, liabilities about 10x assets, recurring losses and shrinking assets. It relies on its sole executive's advances for funding. No litigation was found, and restatement and auditor-change searches returned nothing, but the financial fragility is severe. Research only, not investment advice.

## Financial flags

- **High:** Liabilities ($93,508) are about 10.5x total assets ($8,866) at 2026-04-30; stockholders' deficit of roughly $84,642 (derived). Why: Balance sheet is deeply insolvent on a book basis, and the gap has widened each year.
- **High:** Net losses in all three years (-12,160; -41,049; -27,159) while total assets fell from 49,518 to 8,866. Why: Persistent losses are eroding the small asset base; liquidity is nearly exhausted.
- **Medium:** Revenue is tiny and volatile (21,920 → 16,200 → 27,600) against expenses of $54,759. Why: Operations are not self-sustaining; funding relies on advances from the sole executive or equity.
- **Low:** Cash and long-term debt are not available from the data. Why: Liquidity cannot be fully assessed from structured data; operating cash outflow was $22,859 in FY2026.

## Risks from the 10-K

- **High · financial:** Auditors have issued a going concern opinion; substantial doubt about continuing for the next twelve months. "Our auditors have issued a going concern opinion." [7-p001]
- **High · governance:** Material weakness in internal control: no segregation of duties, no independent directors, no audit committee, no formal review process. "none of whom are independent under applicable standards; we do not have an audit committee or compensation committee." [9A-p011]
- **High · financial:** Dependence on advances from sole executive or equity issuance; no assurance of financing, with dilution risk. "We have financed our operations primarily from either advances from our sole executive or the issuance of equity." [7-p015]
- **Medium · operational:** Start-up with limited operating history and key-person dependence on a sole executive. "We are in start-up stage operations and have generated limited revenues." [7-p018]
- **Medium · financial:** Financing may be unavailable on acceptable terms, which could force the company to curtail operations. "We have no assurance that future financing will be available to us on acceptable terms." [7-p019]

## Open questions for a reviewer

- What are the terms, size and repayment of the sole executive's advances (likely most of the $93,508 liabilities)?
- What is the cash balance? It was not available from structured data.
- Does the company have a realistic plan or funding for the marketing program it says it needs?
- Check Item 9 and the audit report for any auditor change; Item 1A is omitted as a smaller reporting company, so risk coverage is thin.
- Is the business viable beyond the mobile app/website? Check Item 1 and the related-party notes.
